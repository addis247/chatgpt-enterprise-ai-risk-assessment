# Executive Summary

## Business Scenario

Northstar Health Services (fictional organization) is evaluating ChatGPT Enterprise for approved business functions including document drafting, administrative summarization, employee productivity support, and policy development.

The organization requested an AI governance and third-party risk assessment before approval of the technology.

## Assessment Objective

The purpose of this assessment was to evaluate the governance, security, privacy, compliance, and operational risks associated with deploying ChatGPT Enterprise in an enterprise healthcare environment.

## Frameworks Applied

- NIST AI RMF 1.0
- NIST AI 600-1
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5

## Assessment Activities

- AI Risk Assessment
- Third-Party Risk Assessment
- Vendor Security Review
- Risk Register Development
- Governance Evaluation
- Control Mapping
- Executive Risk Reporting

## Key Risks Identified

1. Potential unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information.
2. AI-generated inaccurate or misleading outputs.
3. Prompt injection and misuse risks.
4. Data retention and monitoring limitations.
5. Vendor governance and third-party dependency risks.

## Recommended Controls

- Human review of AI-generated outputs.
- Role-based access control (RBAC).
- Security awareness training.
- Logging and continuous monitoring.
- Data classification and acceptable-use policies.
- Ongoing risk review and governance oversight.

## Final Recommendation

Conditionally Approved

Deployment may proceed provided that identified risks are mitigated through governance controls, access management, monitoring, and documented acceptable-use requirements.
