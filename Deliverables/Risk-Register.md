# Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level | Recommended Mitigation |
|----------|-----------------|------------|---------|------------|----------------------|
| R-001 | Sensitive information disclosure through AI prompts | High | High | High | Data classification policy and user training |
| R-002 | Prompt injection attacks | Medium | High | High | Input validation and monitoring |
| R-003 | AI hallucinations generating inaccurate information | High | Medium | High | Human review of outputs |
| R-004 | Unauthorized user access | Medium | High | High | RBAC and MFA |
| R-005 | Excessive permissions assigned to users | Medium | Medium | Medium | Access reviews and least privilege |
| R-006 | Inadequate monitoring and logging | Medium | High | High | SIEM integration and continuous monitoring |
| R-007 | Vendor dependency and service disruption | Medium | Medium | Medium | Business continuity planning |
| R-008 | Regulatory compliance violations | Medium | High | High | Governance oversight and policy controls |
| R-009 | Data retention conflicts | Medium | Medium | Medium | Defined retention policies |
| R-010 | Third-party security assurance gaps | Medium | Medium | Medium | Annual vendor reassessment |
